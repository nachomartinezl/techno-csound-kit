SUPPORTED SOUND ENGINE
Only these voices exist: kick, bass, closed_hat, open_hat, clap, perc, stab, noise.
Do not invent pad, drone, acid, arp, ride, or supersaw voice names. Build an arpeggio
using pitched stab events; approximate atmosphere using long, quiet stab/noise events.
There are no sample paths, custom opcodes, per-note pan controls, independent resonance
parameters, continuous filter automation, glide controls, or dedicated mastering targets.
tone and drive are static track controls. tone maps differently for each voice.
drive is a LINEAR waveshaper gain: 1 is neutral; it is not a 0..1 intensity knob.
For a firm kick, start around drive 1.2–1.8; values below 1 attenuate its body.
Use track/clip gain_db for musical level. Lower master gain if the mix is too dense.

TIMING AND UNITS
Fixed 4/4, four sixteenth-note steps per beat, 16 steps per bar; bars and steps start at 0.
Pattern event step must be an integer less than pattern.bars * 16.
duration_steps is the total sounding note length in sixteenth-note units, not seconds.
At 140 BPM, one step is about 0.107143 seconds. A useful kick length is 2–3 steps;
closed hats about 0.25–0.6 steps; bass notes about 0.5–1.2 steps.
Pattern MIDI notes are integers. A clip transpose adds semitones to every event.
root_pitch_class uses C=0, C#=1, D=2, Eb=3, E=4, F=5, F#=6, G=7, Ab=8, A=9, Bb=10, B=11.
The declared scale must include interval 0, with no duplicate intervals.
Every bass/stab note AFTER transposition must belong to the declared scale.
Kick notes must be MIDI 24..48. MIDI 31 is about 49 Hz, 32 about 52 Hz, 33 about 55 Hz.
Kick pitch need not belong to the melodic scale. Nonpitched hats/clap/noise may use MIDI 60.
swing delays odd sixteenth steps by swing * step_duration; it is not a tempo change.
Music duration = bars * 4 * 60 / bpm. Render duration adds master.tail_seconds.
At 140 BPM, 105 bars is exactly 180 seconds of music, plus the chosen reverb tail.

ARRANGEMENT RULES
Sections must be ordered and cover exactly bars bars, without gaps or overlaps.
Clips must reference existing track and pattern IDs and end by the final bar.
Clips on the same track cannot overlap. Different tracks may overlap.
A clip repeats its pattern from step 0; a final partial repetition is allowed.
Notes are shortened at the clip end to preserve deliberate breaks.
IDs are unique within tracks and within patterns. Use lowercase letters, digits,
underscores; start with a lowercase letter; keep each ID at most 32 characters.
Declare exactly one kick track and schedule at least one actual kick event.
Keep kick and bass pan at 0, including ramps. For hats and percussion use modest pan.

RAMPS AND MIX RULES
Supported ramps: gain_db, lowpass_hz, highpass_hz, pan, room_send, delay_send.
Each clip can have at most four ramps, one per parameter. from and to must each be
within the parameter's range below. Ramps interpolate at each note onset and hold
that value for the note; a long note will not sweep continuously.
A gain_db ramp replaces the clip gain_db and adds to the track gain_db.
Other ramps replace the corresponding track mix value for that clip.
Combined track and clip gain must not exceed +6 dB at either ramp endpoint.
Keep highpass_hz < lowpass_hz at both endpoints, accounting for BOTH ramps together.
room_send and delay_send are linear send gains, not feedback controls or dB.
room_feedback is the room decay control. echo_decay weights four finite echo taps.
Do not equate schema-valid numbers with a professional mix: leave headroom,
avoid masking, preserve contrast, and use the generated audio for listening checks.
